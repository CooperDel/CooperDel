DESIGN EVOLUTION / 03

My Design

My design from Spring 2026

  • ~70 components (many being washers due to bolt sizing)
  • Simple Assembly - Custom machined part and rib
  • Compact design

CAD Overview

  • The actuator sits inside aluminum tubing with a milled pocket and mounting hole pattern
  • The actuator output mounts directly to the aluminum output block
  • Bolts pass through an aluminum spacer and the tubing to secure the actuator to the aluminum tubing
  • The spacer remains stationary as the output rotates, contacting the thrust washer to transfer load through the actuator housing and tubing, reducing the load on the actuator output

Initial Analysis

Specific points of analysis:

  • Bottom of wheel (contact point with ground) to the bearing in the gearbox
  • Bearing in the gearbox to the bearing in the motor
  • Bearing in the motor to the bearing in the actuator

Force & Stress Analysis

  • Mapped forces through the structure in MATLAB to calculate reaction forces and moments at each bearing
  • Analyzed aluminum tubing for deflection, torsion, buckling, and bending, with deflection limited to maintain swerve accuracy
  • Results showed a comfortable margin of safety for Von Mises stress, deflection below the threshold, and axial load below the critical buckling load

ANSYS

  • Simplified non-essential geometry to reduce element and node count
  • Modeled bolts as beam line bodies with bonded contacts, with imprints along face to simulate load transfer
  • Modeled contacts between bodies as friction contacts
  • Modeled the aluminum output block as a remote displacement to allow 1 DOF along rotation in y-axis
  • Singularity at one bolt hole on gusset plate is disregarded as stress values of ~2000psi match closely to MATLAB produced calculations

Prototype/Testing

Undergoing machining and testing to validate design prior to producing the three more needed to complete the rover drivetrain

  • The custom aluminum output was outsourced for machining (chamfers will be manually added through deburring)
  • The custom rib is in the process of being machined
  • Someone else is working on a testbed to confirm the design and the ANSYS analysis performed
