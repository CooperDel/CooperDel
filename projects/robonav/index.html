RoboNav.

RoboNav is Georgia Tech's Mars rover competition team as part of RoboJackets, which competes in the University Rover Challenge at the Mars Desert Research Facility in Hanksville, Utah. I am a member of the Mechanical sub-team, where I served as Mechanical Lead from 2025-2026.

Mechanical Lead Responsibilities

  • Conduct design reviews with 20+ members in the Mechanical sub-team
  • Define requirements and limitations laid out by the Electrical, Software, and Science teams to apply within the mechanical sub-systems
  • Managed CAD organization and workflow within Kenesto
  • Compiled entire rover BOM to ensure all components were necessary and accounted for during purchasing
  • Point of contact for manufacturers to outsource machining
