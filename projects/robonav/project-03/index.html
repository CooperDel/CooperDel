The Design

  • Stepper drives lead screw to translate laterally across the chassis
  • Elbow and shoulder feature a custom cycloidal gearbox coupled with a CubeMars actuator for the joint pitch
  • Belt driven differential wrist to control pitch and yaw of the end effector

Arm Base

Used this to help drive stepper selection for lead screw and bolt selection for base plate

Stepper Analysis:

  • Used the arm base loading conditions gathered from the full arm analysis to determine the torque needed to move the arm initially
  • Calculated the torque needed to accelerate the arm
  • Applied a Factor of Safety of 1.6 to the addition of the static and dynamic torque requirements to determine requirement of the stepper motor

Bolt Analysis

  • Used the arm base loading conditions gathered from the full arm analysis to determine the axial and shear loads on the bolts
  • Calculated the bolt-joint stiffness ratio to be used in determining the total tensile force on each bolt
  • Evaluated the bolt stress due to the axial, normal, and offset loads, and determined safety through a Margin of Safety calculation
