The Design

V2 design of the NOVA rover:

  • Servo-driven planetary gearbox to control swerve
  • Belt-driven wheels
  • 3D printed drivetrain

Electrical Integration

Core Electronics:

  • Jetson Orin Nano Developer Kit
  • Raspberry Pi Pico 2W
  • Two Cytron motor controllers
  • U2D2 power hub
  • IMU
→

Initially, the wiring was extremely complex, mainly due to the use of a breadboard, as solid-core wires were chosen to better fit into the breadboard, providing very little compliance, forcing the breadboard to remain not flush to the base of the chassis. I then went in and replaced all the solid-core with stranded and added crimps on the ends to fit into the breadboard, though the concern still remains of them falling out during operation over the terrain. Now, after organizing the wires, the BOOTSEL button is accessible on the Pico, as well as the operator can now visibly see all connections, improving serviceability.
