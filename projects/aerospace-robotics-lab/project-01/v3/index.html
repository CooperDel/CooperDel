The Design

V3 design of the NOVA rover:

  • Actuator-driven swerve
  • Wheel mounts directly to actuator
  • Aluminum drivetrain
  • Differential bar in the back

THE DESIGN

New Swerve

→
  • V3 features a design with fewer components, decreasing assembly complexity and time
  • V3 does not have a custom planetary gearbox, reducing slop at the swerve output, improving accuracy enabling testing to be focusing solely on dynamics and other sensors desired
  • V3 does not have a belt-driven wheel, eliminating the possibility of the wheel slipping during operation
  • V3 features a dowel pin clocking feature, where one dowel pin and the plate eliminate five degrees of freedom, and the second dowel pin aligns the angle to allow the bolts to fit through the aluminum output into the aluminum spacer, decreasing the possibility of an angled output due to an assembly error

THE DESIGN

Increased Payload Capacity

  • Payload capacity is increased to 10kg
  • YAM arm (5kg weight + 5kg payload capacity) attached to the chassis to highlight its new capabilities

THE DESIGN

Improved Serviceability

  • Differential bar now mounts on the back, not on the top of the chassis -> allows the back lid to easily be removed to plug in/out the batteries
  • The connectors for the drivetrain actuators are now mounted on the side instead on the top of the chassis -> allows the front lid to easily be removed to inspect and debug custom PCB

THE DESIGN

Overhauled Electrical

Custom PCB:

  • Incorporates power distribution from the battery to the four actuators (each leg is daisy-chained)
  • STM32 microcontroller, switching from the Pico in V2
  • Handles CAN communication for the actuators to the microcontroller
  • BNO085 IMU is included, communicating to the microcontroller via I2C
