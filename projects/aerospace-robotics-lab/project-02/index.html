Aerospace Robotics Lab / CASE STUDY

UUV.

Objective

• Address a gap in the welding industry, as there is a lack of skilled labor within the underwater welding profession

Ownership

• Received funding for personal idea • Manage budget and purchasing hardware • Complete mechanical design • Software simulation along with partner

Approach

• Use Pinocchio to perform inverse dynamic analysis to spec actuators • Perform CAD in Onshape • Use OceanSim, an IsaacSim plug-in, to model sensors in an aquatic environment and implement reinforcement learning

  • First modeled the arm in RViz to ensure proper coordinate system alignment and develop closed-loop constraints so the solver can mathematically keep the end effector platform "controlled" by three children joints
  • Applied a buoyancy, drag, and quadratic drag force at each linkage, as well as modeled inertial effects

  • Positioned forces relative to the arm orientation (upside down or right-side up) to account for actuator requirements in any of the anticipated orientations during testing

The Design

Arm:

  • Each joint features a dynamic seal to prevent water intrusion from generating unnecessary friction
  • Actuators feature an o-ring gasket to ensure the enclosure will not leak while underwater
  • The top end effector contains a marker held in place by set screws → this marker tip will simulate the welding tip

Vehicle:

  • All cameras also feature o-ring gaskets at both the back and underneath the acrylic dome
  • The vehicle is built around ROV Maker components, including the acrylic tube and wire seals at the back
  • The thrusters feature a yaw joint from servos

Electronics Overview:

  • My partner designed a custom flight controller and BMS which will be mounted to our battery pack
  • Main compute is through the Jetson Orin Nano Super and communicates to the actuators via the CAN transceiver
  • Four ESCs to control the four thrusters

Assembly Overview:

  • The Electronics Bay will slide into the acrylic tube and bolt onto one end of the tube plate

CFD

Modeled the UUV with an external flow simulation in ANSYS Fluent

  • Utilized two Bodies of Interest to maximize mesh complexity whilst minimizing node and element amount adhering to the ANSYS student license restrictions
  • Skewness Average of 0.05335 Orthogonal Average of 0.92480 to underscore a well converged mesh
  • Found center of pressure offset from center of mass and determined thruster placement and thrust was sufficient to correct the pressure moment
