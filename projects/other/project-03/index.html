ME 2110 Competition.

Objective

• Score as many points as possible across 4 metrics to win the ME 2110 competition

Ownership

• Design, implementation, and iteration of the drawer slide sample collection mechanism

Approach

• Test mechanisms across several benchmarks across the semester, validating a new mechanism each time

DESIGN EVOLUTION / 01

The Design

  • Drawer slide mechanism actuated by a pneumatic actuator and pulled back via a string+pulley system connected to a DC motor
  • Telescoping arm mechanism actuated via looping string throughout PVC pipes
  • "Slingshot" mechanism actuates via pushing force caused by rotating center and elastic energy stored via rubber band to launch ping pong balls

Customr Interaction

  • We determined customer requirements based on the competition guidelines
  • We framed the engineering requirements based on both the competition guidelines and internal requirements set forth to succeed in the competition
  • We paired the customer and engineering requirements into a House of Quality to better visualize which relationships were most important, driving engineering decisions within design and manufacturing

Design Conceptualization

  • Used a morph chart to conceptualize certain design regarding the drawer slide mechanism and sample collection
  • Modeled some of the ideas generated from the morph chart in SolidWorks, and compared overall assembly to various criteria spanning a mix of customer and engineering requirements

Competition

We placed 4th out of 66 teams in the Fall 2024 ME 2110 Competition at Georgia Tech
