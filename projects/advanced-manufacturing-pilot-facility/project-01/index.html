Schlieren Set-Up.

Objective

• Determine whether Argon flow was interfering with the metal powder flow at the nozzle in an Optomec printer

Ownership

• The design of the rail system • Determining the COTS Schlieren kit

Approach

• Understand how a Schlieren system works • Take field measurements inside Optomec printer

This project utilizes a COTS Schlieren setup to place inside an Optomec printer to understand how the Argon flow within the machine interferes with the powder flow at the nozzle. The design incorporates a large lazy susan, enabling full 360 degree rotationw ith set orientations at every ~13 degrees, where the top rails will be locked into place using dowel pins. The mirror would mount inside its casing, where three bolts would tighten down springs to properly align the mirror orientation across the Schlieren setup. The knife would refract the light at the initial source as well as right before the camera.
