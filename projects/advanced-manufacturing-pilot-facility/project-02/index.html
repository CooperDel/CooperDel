Overview

This design features a 3-axis rail system, where the 80-20 can translate in both the x and y-axes. There is then a jack used to mvoe the camera in the z-axis. Lastly, there is an x,y-plate for fine translation to finalize the position of the camera na dorient the test subject in the mechanical tester perfectly in view. In front of the longer rail system is a support tower for the camera tubing, in the chance there is a large amount of tubing needed. The Mechanical Tester is positioned in between the two rail systems. This design was paired with a custom calculator to determine amount of camera tubing and lens orientation based upon camera distance to subjkect and what area of the subject was desired to capture.
